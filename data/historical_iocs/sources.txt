Spoof Spotter Historical IOC Sources
====================================

Source: Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) / IC3
Dataset: Phishing Domains Associated with LabHost PhaaS Platform Users
Publication date: 2025-04-29
Classification: TLP:CLEAR

Purpose:
Historical IOC correlation for defensive cybersecurity analysis.

Dataset context:
The FBI released approximately 42,000 phishing domains associated with
the LabHost phishing-as-a-service platform between November 2021 and
April 2024.

Important limitations:
- The information is historical in nature.
- A listed domain may no longer be malicious.
- FBI states that not every domain in the dataset was individually validated.
- Inclusion in this dataset must not be treated as proof of current maliciousness.
- Spoof Spotter uses this dataset as historical security context only.
